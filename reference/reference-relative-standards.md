# Reference-relative standards

Methods, definitions and invariants can transfer; subject measurements cannot. Judge each caller-selected character against its own declared reference, baselines and limits. No historical character tables, palettes, landmarks, measured native matrices or body ranges are bundled. The independent board/cloth example is not a human population sample.

| Area | Generic contract | Explicit caller data |
|---|---|---|
| geometry | normalized landmark definitions, mirror coverage with error, welded incidence, UV ownership/gutter, area-weighted density | body scope, pose, landmarks, correspondence, delivery budgets and tolerances |
| painted texture | named color space, selected patch statistics, bounded support, exact protected pixels | palette, eligible regions, measured targets, uncertainty, padding footprint |
| rig | actual skin joint order, inverse binds, world-delta rebase, quaternion sign continuity | skeleton semantics, rest placement, clips, speeds and independent control |
| weights | normalized finite influence rows, monotone chains, explicit mirror permutations, fixed-triangle strain | transition regions, meaningful pose sweep and own baseline |
| hair | root attachment, seam continuity and approved-exception comparison | chain layout, pin height, spring settings and independently reviewed exception baseline |

`reference-within`, `no-worse-than-baseline`, `declared-min` and `declared-max` require finite provenance-bearing declarations. Wildcards expand over declared keys as well as measured keys so omitting a pose/region cannot yield a pass. Advisory results remain visible but do not change the required-gate status. Missing required measurements never pass.

Declare the target's reference/baselines/limits before authoring. S2B, S9, S10 and S11 fragments and their independent measurers support texture, topology/UV, rig/pose and hair respectively. They do not edit the input. Source admission, native/material/parity/G10 rules remain independent and unchanged.

Known limits include camera-calibrated landmarks, posed self-intersection, continuous hair collision and rendered material fidelity; see the individual measurement docs. No unknown receives an invented measurement or legal clearance claim.
