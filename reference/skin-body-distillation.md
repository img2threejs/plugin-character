# Skin/body workflow with caller-owned data

This route retains generic texture, topology, weight, mask, head/neck and hair tools. Historical subject studies and their measurements are not shipped. The only bundled catalogue is a project-authored geometric primitive inventory, not a human baseline. Real native and CC5 workflows remain supported and require their own admitted inputs.

1. Admit sources with identity, hashes, provenance, permission and attribution. A hash or rights string does not establish legal permission.
2. Select an explicit catalogue, reference/baseline files, body scope, coordinate frame and neutral regions. Missing measurements remain unknown/unmeasured.
3. Preserve original files and protected pixels/geometry. Work on copies. Measure tone in explicitly selected encoded-sRGB or linear light; these are different operations.
4. Author texture only inside declared support. Measure real UV coverage, padding and protected-pixel preservation; do not fix geometry with a global recolor.
5. Join caller-selected boundaries while preserving supplied corner attributes and independently matched source correspondence. A local manifold strip is not proof of whole-body watertightness.
6. Supply actual skeleton semantics, mirror pairs and bone permutations. Preserve normalized weights with at most four retained influences; treat unmatched/ambiguous pairs as unmeasured, never clean symmetry.
7. Author neutral masks from explicit eligibility and dimensions. No historical coordinates, anatomical proportions or artistic caps are reusable measurements.
8. Bind hair with caller-selected chains, widths, root pins and spring parameters; validate root and seam constraints and compare actual motion. No collision guarantee is implied.
9. Measure saved native artifacts and independently decoded exports. Native shader settings do not prove browser SSS, pores, material parity or rendered quality.
10. Execute the fail-closed S1–S11 contracts and inspect actual native/browser behavior. G10, material, native-control and parity rules are not waived by a synthetic example.

`skin_body_plan.py` requires a `catalogueFile` and selected asset ID and forwards them consistently. `examples/skin-body/plan.example.json` is a plan-input template, not generated assets or acceptance evidence. See `skin-body-agent-contract.md`, `skin-texture.md`, `skin-atlas-authoring.md`, `body-parts.md`, and `head-hair-authoring.md` for interfaces and limitations.

The independently authored board/cloth scene is documented in `garment-character-route.md`. It exercises geometric mechanics only. Removing case data from HEAD does not erase history, grant rights or constitute legal approval; see `../NOTICE`.
