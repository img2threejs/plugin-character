# Skin atlas authoring

Use `tools/atlas_surface.py` on bottom-up pixel arrays after the geometry and UV cuts are established. The original body must be opaque uint8, W by W/2, W divisible by eight. `composite_atlas` copies those bytes exactly; donor squares of W/2 are copied or half-sized squares are 2× resampled. The strip is reserved for a separately baked bridge. [behaviour; case: job `native/transplant_head.py:447-488`]

The composite is W by 9W/8. Remap body `(u,4v/9)`, donor face `(u/2,4/9+4v/9)`, and donor neck `(u/2+1/2,4/9+4v/9)`. [behaviour; same case, `native/transplant_head.py:759-773`] The case had 2048×2304 and 4096×4608 versions; these are single-subject examples, not prescribed output sizes. [single-subject; job `README.md:113`]

For a ring, sample at column angles `2π(i+.5)/W`; `neck_strip` accepts owner-edge UVs and their source atlas for interpolation and repeat-bilinear lookup, or pre-sampled colors. Decode stored sRGB to linear, blend with `t=clamp((row+.5-pad)/(count-2pad))`, `t²(3-2t)`, then encode and quantize. `pad=4W/2048`; endpoints are `(strip_start+pad)/height` and `(strip_end-pad)/height`, so the 2048 and 4096 case UVs coincide. [behaviour; case `native/transplant_head.py:382-403,475-488`] Padding must leave a positive blend interval. The caller must integrate the strip and validate the resulting join; these image functions do not weld geometry.

For bounded source-color repair, pass explicit measured shadow, clean skin, normalized top-down box, residual gate and edge feather widths. Project each stored-sRGB color onto the shadow-to-skin line; multiply the projection amount by smoothstep chromatic and spatial gates. All pixels outside the box remain byte-identical; `assert_zero_change_outside_mask` checks that boundary independently. [behaviour; case `native/nape_atlas.py:10-37`] Example only: shadow `[231,173,141]`, skin `[246,203,177]`, box `[.700,.018,.871,.205]`, residual `.018`, fade `.010`, U feather `(.010,.006)`, V `(.010,.010)`. [single-subject; same case] Historical receipt: 26,687 pixels changed, zero outside and untouched error 0.0; this is evidence for that asset, not this tool's current output. [single-subject; `evidence/nape-08-blue-native.json:9-44`]

Do not globally recolor the donor to hide a seam. The case donor neck was 2.6–2.7 cm rearward with a hidden flare; rigid alignment and a cut addressed geometry rather than face reshaping. [single-subject; job `README.md:112`] Hairline paint repair was a separate original-paint mask and spatial eligibility operation, with eight-pixel extension and 400 neighbor-fill iterations; it is not a generic color-line repair.
