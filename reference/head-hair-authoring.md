# Head transplant and connected hair authoring

All distances are metres. These pure NumPy tools accept subject-specific input and do not install a Blender rig, export assets, or verify a rendered result. Confirm source asset rights and inspect the actual fit and deformation. `native/...` references below refer to the read-only showcase job, not files required by these modules.

## Placement and topology

1. Measure donor hair maximum world Z. Apply `fit_similarity(max_z, scale, crown_z, y_translation)` to **every** donor part in the same world frame. The 4×4 transform uses `tz = crown_z - scale*max_z`. [single-subject] Scale 0.043, crown 1.7565430402755737 and Y −0.028, body cut 1.515 and donor cut 1.555 (`native/head-transplant-parameters.json:3`; `native/transplant_head.py:730-735,755-774`). These are not defaults.
2. Clip each polygon via `clip_polygon_plane` with corner arrays `position`, UV, normal and weight arrays. Intersections interpolate every supplied attribute. Normalize normals downstream as needed; interpolated weights require downstream pruning/normalization. Position-only welding in `boundary_rings` is temporary topology analysis, not a mesh weld. Supply the cut-boundary polygon set; inspect each returned loop. Original implementation identifies cut-plane edges explicitly (`native/transplant_head.py:174-177,273-316`).
3. `bridge_rings` angle-merges ordered star-shaped rings. The returned vertices and triangle indices describe the strip; the report counts each strip boundary edge with one assumed adjoining surface incidence. Counts are **not** an audit of real adjoining meshes: caller must verify their boundary correspondence and weld positions at 1 µm. It reports count and angular mismatch and XY centre offset; those are diagnostics, not an automatic fit decision. Non-star-shaped rings are rejected. [behaviour] The source bridges by angular merge (`native/transplant_head.py:319-367`). [single-subject] 96 triangles, 192 edges, zero open/nonmanifold join edges at 1 µm (`evidence/head-10-blue-fidelity.json:9-20,45-56`). A manifold strip does not prove a good fit.
4. Provide an explicit skin-eligibility mask to `cheek_masks`; missing eligibility fails closed. Estimate cheek centre from eye position: `cx=1.30*eye_x`, `cz=eye_z-.55*span`, radii `[.55*eye_x,.70*eye_x,.22*span]`. Median Y comes from the landmark window. Exclude nose, above-iris, non-skin and opposite-side vertices. Each side needs at least eight active vertices (`native/transplant_head.py:644-675`). [single-subject] These landmark factors are starting values, not universal anatomy.

## Hair binding

This is a **separate explicit route** from `tools/rig_geodesic_skinning.py`, not a change to its policy. Weld seam coordinates with `weld_seams`, remap mesh edges to welded indices, classify below-pin connected components with `classify_front_locks`, and derive caller-selected chain centre lines with `chain_layout`. The classifier assumes the dominant component is the rear curtain and every other below-pin component is in front and clearly sided; it rejects ambiguous inputs. It is not semantic segmentation. Minimum bone length is 0.01 m (`native/donor_hair_rig.py:186-214`).

Supply descending band centres, descending per-chain bone-centre heights, a positive half-width profile, face chain heights and pin/blend to `hair_weights`. Lateral coordinate is `x/halfWidth(z)`; adjacent bands and bone heights use quintic `u³(10−15u+6u²)`. Head share is `1−smooth((pin−z)/blend)`. Weights below 1e-4 are pruned and the rest renormalized; more than four influences are rejected instead of silently discarding weights (`native/donor_hair_rig.py:132-183,285-309`). Map unique rows back using the returned seam inverse. `validate_binding` rejects unnormalized rows, excessive influences, unequal seam weights, and missing or unpinned roots. [single-subject] Five rear chains of four bones, two face chains of two, pin 1.63, blend .05, band centres .8,.4,0,−.4,−.8 (`native/donor_hair_rig.py:67-89`).

## Motion and preservation

Carry rest targets into world space from sampled Head transforms before `simulate_chain`. Fixed-root damped springs integrate semi-implicit substeps and project each segment back to its initial length; explicit substep count must satisfy `max(omega)*dt/substeps <= .2` or it raises. `shape_quaternion` operates on a caller-computed **local** quaternion in `[w,x,y,z]` order, with radians, producing `cap*tanh(amplitude*angle/cap)` and snapping negligible rotations. The caller is responsible for deriving local rotations and writing keys. Compare complete channel mappings using `assert_non_hair_channels_unchanged` and an explicit set of hair channel names. [single-subject] Rear 2.6→1.8 Hz, damping .5, cap 3°; face 3.6→3.2 Hz, damping .6, cap .25°; strength .25 (`native/bake_hair_motion.py:38-45,278-323`; `native/donor_hair_rig.py:94`). These are calibration values only. **There is no collision solver**; spring motion and fixed lengths do not establish clearance (`native/bake_hair_motion.py:8-13`).

## Failure evidence and limits

[single-subject] The donor neck was 2.6–2.7 cm rearward and had a coat-hidden flare; rigidly align and cut the flare rather than deforming the face (`README.md:112`). Hair09 candidates were rejected for exposed roots, helmet silhouette, lumpy ear even after a physical pass, and exposed old scalp paint (`evidence/hair-09-design-iterations.json:4-26`). A geometric validator cannot replace visual review. None of these modules measures collision, surface correspondence, donor face fidelity, or complete exported animation parity.
