# Caller catalogues and the synthetic primitive inventory

`body-parts.json` is generated by the repository-maintenance script `scripts/generate_synthetic_reference.py` from the independent board example. It describes the actual `SupportBoard`, `ClothGrid` and single `BoardAnchor` bone built by `runtime/garment-character/synthetic-scene.mjs`. It is not an anatomical skeleton. The fabric part is present; anatomical head/hair/torso/abdomen are unknown, not measured or asserted absent. No old body measurements or rig counts are retained.

`tools/body_parts.py` validates `body-parts/1`, evaluates explicit ellipsoidal masks and normalizes overlap by `max(1,sum)`. It compares caller-supplied mirrored weight rows through an explicit bone permutation and reports unmatched/ambiguous pairs. Structural validation is not verification of a measurement, citation or permission.

Real workflows supply their own catalogue to `bpy_measure_skin_body.py --catalogue FILE --character ASSET_ID`. The selector must identify exactly one asset; required semantic bone groups must be supplied and resolved. `skin_body_plan.py` requires `catalogueFile` and passes that selected path and `catalogueCharacterId` consistently to every produced skin/body measurement command and task input. The default primitive catalogue does not supply missing human measurements.

A catalogue records asset identity, stage, coordinate basis, rights statement, bones/parents/mirrors, meshes, part status, semantic groups and optional explicitly cited measurements/masks/profiles. Unknown and absent are distinct. Do not invent skeletal parentage or treat selected representatives as a complete inventory. Neutral skin-body region policy remains unchanged; a fabric mechanics example does not authorize anatomical authoring.

The synthetic configuration's nine soft-region IDs are solver vocabulary for arbitrary grid patches, not anatomical claims. No anatomy, original asset fidelity, donor rights or native acceptance is established. See `../NOTICE`.
