# Body parts, skin texture and weights

`body-parts.json` is a selected, versioned case catalogue, not a universal skeleton. `tools/body_parts.py` is a pure NumPy supplement; it does not generate texture, install a rig, or establish likeness. Source references beginning `robin-bikini-transfer-01/` and `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/` identify the respective local job. Numbers below are **single-subject** unless labelled **behaviour**.

## Identity, stages and parts

The body and feet derive from fatymazali's Nami model under CC BY 4.0, with attribution; franchise rights are not established. Robin's user-supplied donor head and hair have no established creator or licence, and no public redistribution permission is asserted. The historical Nami control asset has four joints; the later Nami anatomical showcase has 53. Robin's full rig has 80 bones; the preserved head component has 28 (four original controls and 24 hair bones). These are different stages, not interchangeable counts. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/ATTRIBUTION.txt:4-13,19-25`; `robin-bikini-transfer-01/README.md:101-115`; `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/donor_hair_rig.py:3-4,61-62`]

The selected catalogue links head/face, neck, torso, abdomen and waist, arms and hands, legs and feet, and hair to mesh components and representative bones. Fingers and toes are represented in the anatomical skeleton by bilateral finger chains and toe bases/end markers; the catalogue does not pretend its representative bone list is exhaustive. The central chain includes Root, Pelvis, Spine, Spine1/Spine2, Neck and Head; bilateral groups include Shoulder, Arm, ForeArm, Hand, five three-segment finger chains, UpLeg, Leg, Foot, ToeBase, and Robin toe end markers. Nami showcase joints use the `Nami_` prefix, and its face and hair follow Head rigidly; the historical four-joint rig has no individual finger or limb articulation. [behaviour: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/evidence/nami-native-construction.json:467-525`; `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/nami-showcase/build_nami.py:13-23,1384-1388`; `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/ATTRIBUTION.txt:21-22`]

Native coordinates are metres, +Z up, front −Y and anatomical left +X; glTF coordinates are `(x,z,-y)`, Y up and front +Z. Null parents in a selected inventory do not prove a root relationship. Explicit absent and unknown are distinct. [behaviour: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/pinoc/anatomy-landmarks.json:2-4`; `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/native/prepare_character.py:72-76`]

## Skin texture is required

Weights do not replace head identity or skin texture. Plan each target's actual UVs, identity, pixel provenance and rights. Nami's artist-painted shared sRGB atlas is 2048×1024; the face UV bounding box is `[0.8817,0.0197]–[0.9957,0.4824]`, with face density approximately 2,051 px/m versus 558 px/m below the neck. Painted features and shading are not measured skin reflectance; mirrored UVs constrain independent asymmetric detail. No normal map or browser scattering texture is established by this export. [single-subject: `reference/nami-construction-study.md:81-98`]

Use the existing `tools/atlas_surface.py`, `tools/head_transplant.py` and `reference/skin-atlas-authoring.md`: preserve original body pixels, place donor face and neck and reserve a bridge strip. For an opaque original W×W/2 atlas the composite is W×9W/8; body UV `(u,4v/9)`, face `(u/2,4/9+4v/9)`, neck `(u/2+1/2,4/9+4v/9)`. Decode sRGB to linear light for bridge interpolation, then encode and verify actual atlas pixels and export UVs independently. The cases use 2048×2304 and 4096×4608 composites. Donor licence remains unknown; no image is included in this catalogue. [behaviour: `reference/skin-atlas-authoring.md:3-7`; single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/README.md:112-113`]

## Weight authoring and neutral masks

Retain compatible inherited weights, protect donor correspondence and coincident boundaries; relax authored shoulder fields for 80 graph iterations and neck fields for 6, with protected boundaries. The lower-chain redistribution is continuous and preserves each side's incoming mass instead of separately relaxing asymmetrically triangulated feet. Transfer unmatched lower-leg points by nearest-surface barycentric interpolation with side-aware BVHs; propagate unmatched neck/head weights from matched boundaries and blend toward Head. Keep the strongest four weights, dropping values **below** 1e−4 and renormalizing; the earlier preparation path drops values **at or below** 1e−4. Position correspondence does not imply UV correspondence. [behaviour: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/pinoc/repair_actor_skinning.py:124-145,156-226`; `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/nami-showcase/build_nami.py:13-23,65-74,300-366`; `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/native/prepare_character.py:168-174`]

On 681 matched foot pairs the maximum mirrored weight discrepancy fell 0.535104 → 1.99676e−6, and posed mirrored distance 0.0507933 → 1.10521e−6 m. This is a foot-pair receipt, not a full-body guarantee. Nami parity sampling reported maximum surface 1.23205e−5 m and weight 5.96e−8, with 1,634 ambiguous rest-position/UV matches; ambiguity is not a pass. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/evidence/anatomy-skinning-aligned-comparison-02.json:15343-15350,30691-30698`; `robin-bikini-transfer-01/evidence/nami-parity-summary.json:96-155`]

The Nami neutral masks use glTF ellipsoids: abdomen centre `(0,1.21,0.065)`, radii `(0.085,0.13,0.06)`; waist centres `(±0.078,1.19,0)`, radii `(0.055,0.095,0.09)`; cheek centres `(±0.043,1.575,0.047)`, radii `(0.023,0.021,0.030)` metres. Abdomen and waist bind Spine; cheeks bind Head. Cheeks exclude nose and forehead. These are source-bbox-calibrated, not general anatomy. Robin cheek masks instead derive from her own donor landmarks and eligible skin; don't reuse Nami centres. [single-subject: `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/native/prepare_character.py:61-79`; behaviour: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/transplant_head.py:644-675`]

For `q=Σ((p−c)/r)²`, `u=max(0,1−q)`, evaluate `w=u³(10−15u+6u²)` after converting axes; divide all active weights by `max(1,Σw)`. The optional predicate operates on the converted point. Runtime profiles `(frequency Hz, damping ratio, cap m, compression, pressDepth m)` are abdomen `(2.8,0.30,0.014,0.06,0.008)`, waist `(5,0.6,0.006,0.10,absent)`, cheek `(4.5,0.80,0.0025,0,-0.0012)`. **Absent is not zero**; these are artistic parameters, not biological constants. [behaviour: `nami-sketchfab-8e496e5a/native/prepare_character.py:72-79,180-190`; single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/softbody.mjs:13-16`]

## Failure evidence

- The donor neck was 2.6–2.7 cm rearward and had a lower flare; rigid placement and clipping fixed geometry rather than recolouring the face. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/README.md:110-113`]
- The original knee at 0.47475 m lay in the calf, whereas section constriction was 0.60–0.62 m. The replacement is an authored target, not recovered anatomy. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/native/pinoc/anatomy-landmarks.json:12-17`]
- A sampled triangle stretched 17.1182×; continuous lower-chain redistribution reduced the same triangle to 1.009584×. Remaining deformation limitations still require inspection. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/evidence/anatomy-skinning-commands.json:40-49`]
- Quaternion antipodal consecutive pairs were repaired 92 → 0 by sign alignment; that alone does not prove animation correctness. [single-subject: `robin-bikini-transfer-01/evidence/nami-quaternion-repair.json:74-78`]
- A separate overlapping foot shell was rejected; the later local zipper is not a whole-mesh weld. [single-subject: `reference/robin-transfer-study.md:32-34`]
